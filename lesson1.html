How to Budget Your Paychecks

Step-by-Step Budgeting Guide

How do you budget your paychecks? Below we have aligned steps for you take to handle your paychecks.

Lets begin!


  • First, figure out how often you receive a paycheck, whether weekly, biweekly, or monthly, etc. This amount of money is called the gross income.
  • If taxes are deducted automatically, then you can proceed to the next steps. If taxes or any other deductions are not automatically removed from your paycheck, you can deduct all expenses towards taxes and other deductions.
  • After taxes and all deductions are taken out, understand how much money you are left with. This amount of money is called the net income.
  • Write a list of all monthly costs to understand your personal expenses that are consistently the same each month.
  • From the net income, you will allocate the monthly expenses amount. You can turn this into a percentage to see how much of your monthly expenses are deducted from your paycheck.
  • After taking out the monthly expenses amount, calculate what is left.
  • From what is left, decide which goal, between savings and spending, is more important.
  • Assign a percentage to each, whether 50% each, 60-40%, or any percentage that suits you, with the goal you would like to achieve quicker as the highest amount.
  • You can also add other categories of savings into this tier as desired, adjusting the percentage of savings.
  • You are finally having money budgeted from your paycheck into monthly expenses, savings, and spending.


Quick Tips

  • Categorize Bills:
    Group them by "Fixed" (Rent) vs. "Variable" (Groceries) to predict your monthly baseline.
  • The 48-Hour Review:
    Check your bank app every 2 days to catch "spending leaks" while they're fresh.
  • Save $500:
    Set aside just $42 a week to hit your $500 goal in 3 months!
