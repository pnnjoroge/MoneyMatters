Ready to Take Control of Your Finances?

Building your financial foundation can start at any age, at any amount earned, at any stage of life, and with any level of experience!


Before Getting Started

Before diving into any topics, ask your self "What are my financial goals that I would like to accomplish?". This way, you can note which topics you would like to learn about and implement to check off your list. We recommend that you only tackle one topic at a time and incorporate the steps adviced before moving on to a new topic. This way, all this information does not overload and overwhelm you. Lets get started!

Getting Started

To get started, we advice that you gather several things. These are:

  • Paystubs or bank statements
  • Take home pay or Net Income
  • List all your bills and their amounts

All of these are going to be for the previous 2 to 3 months, so as to get a good idea of your spending.

Quick Tips

  • Categorize Bills:
    Group them by "Fixed" (Rent) vs. "Variable" (Groceries) to predict your monthly baseline.
  • The 48-Hour Review:
    Check your bank app every 2 days to catch "spending leaks" while they're fresh.
  • Save $500:
    Set aside just $42 a week to hit your $500 goal in 3 months!
