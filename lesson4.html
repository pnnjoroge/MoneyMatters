How to Increase Your Income

Actionable Ways to Boost Your Earnings

How do you increase your income? Here are a few ways to grow your money. You can start by:


  • Negotiating your current job salary increase (if you get one) by researching current market rates and presenting a record of your recent workplace achievements.
  • Picking up extra work shifts or workload if possible at your current job.
  • Signing up to work and offer ride-share services like Uber, UberEats, DoorDash on weekends or on free time to earn some money.
  • Obtaining high-demand certifications in fields of your choice to qualify for higher-paying roles.
  • Monetizing unused assets by renting out your car on Turo or listing extra storage space on Neighbor to earn some extra cash.
  • Launching a digital product store on Etsy to sell templates, planners, e-books or any other products you can create for passive income.
  • Offering specialized freelance services using AI tools to increase your output in areas like copywriting or graphic design.
  • Joining a service-based platform like Poplin for mobile laundry or Sharetown for furniture reselling to earn quick cash.
  • Investing in dividend-paying stocks or ETFs to build a recurring stream of passive wealth over time.
  • Developing a niche online course using your professional expertise to reach a global audience.

There are but a few ways one can earn extra money. Think about other suitable ways you can earn extra money for yourself.
