How to Pay Off Debt

One Of The Many Strategies for Debt Repayment

How do you pay off debt? Lets take a look!


  • Begin by listing all your debts by writing down every balance and interest rate you owe to get a clear, honest picture of your total debt.
  • Pick a Strategy: Decide if you want to target the smallest balance first for a quick win or the highest interest rate to save money.
  • Pay the Minimums by making the minimum payments on every debt to protect your credit score and avoid expensive late fees.
  • Attack One Debt by focusing all your extra cash on your single target debt to watch that balance drop as fast as possible.
  • Roll It Over: Once a debt is gone, add its entire monthly payment to the next one on your list to create a "power payment."
  • Stop the Bleeding and stick to using cash or your debit card so you don't accidentally add new charges while trying to hit zero.

Quick Tips

  • Categorize Bills:
    Group them by "Fixed" (Rent) vs. "Variable" (Groceries) to predict your monthly baseline.
  • The 48-Hour Review:
    Check your bank app every 2 days to catch "spending leaks" while they're fresh.
  • Save $500:
    Set aside just $42 a week to hit your $500 goal in 3 months!
