About Me


I am currently a student balancing the demands of my education with a real-world journey through the complexities of personal finance. Like many people, I am not just observing the financial world from the sidelines; I am actively navigating it while managing my own debt. My transition into financial literacy didn't stem from a textbook, but from the necessity of facing my own financial struggles and setting clear, actionable goals to overcome them.

My journey began when I realized that earning money is only one half of the equation; understanding how to keep, grow, and manage it is where true stability lies. In the beginning, the world of interest rates, credit scores, and budgeting felt like a foreign language. However, as I began implementing specific strategies to tackle my debt, I discovered that financial health is built on small, consistent habits rather than overnight miracles. These strategies—ranging from aggressive debt repayment plans to mindful spending—have become the foundation of my own financial path.

Over time, I have learned that money is a tool that requires constant study. The more I uncover, the more I realize just how much there is yet to explore. Financial education is a lifelong process of growth, and it is a field that remains relevant regardless of your current age or income level. Whether you are a student like me, a mid-career professional, or someone looking toward retirement, the principles of financial growth are universal. There is always room to refine your approach, optimize your savings, and build a more secure future.

I created this space to document what I’ve learned and the methods I’m currently using to improve my own bottom line. I believe that by being transparent about my struggles and my progress, I can provide a roadmap or a spark of inspiration for others facing similar hurdles. Finances can often feel like a private, heavy burden, but opening up the conversation makes the mountain feel a lot easier to climb.

There is so much room to grow, and my goal is to continue expanding my knowledge while helping others do the same. I hope that by sharing my experiences and the lessons I’ve gathered along the way, you can find something valuable to apply to your own life. We are all on a unique financial path, but we don't have to walk it alone. I hope you can learn something from my journey as we grow together toward financial independence.


Sincerely,

Paulette Njoroge
